# Web TV Hub — Product & Strategy Playbook (v2)

> v2, 2026-11-17. Tightened from the original playbook (v1). Every change from v1 is marked **[v2]** with the reason.
> Facts about free tiers were checked against Cloudflare's docs on this date; re-check before relying on them.

## 1. Product vision

A free, web-native, "lean-back" TV site that turns iptv-org's open channel directory and public program guides into a clean, zero-setup cable-TV-style experience for Smart TVs, budget streaming sticks (e.g. Xiaomi TV Stick) and ordinary browsers. Instead of dumping thousands of raw links on the viewer, the site curates, health-checks, categorizes and personalizes a small set of channels that actually play.

**[v2] Positioning in one line:** *a curated TV guide and player for channels that broadcasters already stream for free* — not "free cable".

## 2. Why people would choose it

- **Lean-back:** opens straight into a playing channel and a channel grid; no searching, no choice paralysis.
- **Zero setup:** no .m3u or .xml files to hunt for, no dead links to skip.
- **Clean and safe:** no pop-ups, crypto-miners, domain-hopping or sketchy re-streams (see §5 source policy).
- **Schedule context:** Previous show, live now and the next two shows on every channel that has guide data.
- **Works on any TV with an HDMI stick:** full D-pad navigation in TV browsers (TV Bro, BrowseHere).

## 3. $0 tech stack — corrected

**[v2] Main correction:** the v1 plan ran the 6-hourly refresh in a Cloudflare Worker cron. On the Workers Free plan a Worker gets **10 ms CPU** and **50 subrequests per invocation**, so it cannot health-check thousands of streams or parse multi-MB XMLTV guide files. The refresh job therefore runs in **GitHub Actions** (free for public repos), and its output is static JSON.

| Layer | v2 choice | Free-tier facts (checked 2026-11-17) |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting | **GitHub Pages** now; **Cloudflare Pages** later (same static files) | Pages: free for public repos. Cloudflare Pages: free static hosting. |
| 6-hour refresh, health check, EPG | **GitHub Actions** scheduled workflow (`0 */6 * * *`) | Public repo: free minutes. Scheduled runs can be delayed at busy times and are paused after 60 days of no repo activity. |
| Channel + guide data | **Static JSON** generated by the job (`data/channels.json`, `data/epg.json`) | No database needed for the free core. |
| User profile, favorites, reminders, BYO playlist | **Browser localStorage** | Private by default; no accounts, no personal data on a server. |
| Later: cloud sync, Telegram/Discord alerts, Web Push | **Cloudflare Worker + D1** (only when Pro features ship) | Workers Free: 100k requests/day, 5 cron triggers/account. D1 Free: 5M rows read/day, 100k rows written/day, 5 GB total. Since 2026-09-01 D1 queries **fail** past the daily limit until 00:00 UTC. |
| AI onboarding | **[v2] Client-side tag matching first** (no AI call needed). Optional later: Workers AI | Workers AI Free: 10,000 neurons/day; some large models now require Workers Paid. |

## 4. Data sources and licensing — corrected

- **iptv-org API** (`channels.json`, `streams.json`, `feeds.json`, `categories.json`, `blocklist.json`, `logos.json`).
- **[v2] What The Unlicense covers:** iptv-org's *code and metadata* (channel names, categories, the list of links). It does **not** grant any rights to the *video streams* the links point to; each stream belongs to its broadcaster or distributor. Our legal footing comes from only playing streams the rights-holder publishes for free, and from honoring iptv-org's `blocklist.json` (channels removed on legal or NSFW grounds).
- **Guide (EPG) data [v2]:** iptv-org's `guides.json` only maps channels to guide *sites*; it contains no programme listings. v2 ingests XMLTV from configurable sources. The first source is the free XMLTV published by i.mjh.nz for Pluto TV and Samsung TV Plus. It is a community-run service with no SLA and no formal license, so we treat it as a replaceable dependency.

## 5. Features — what changed

### A. Curation and strict filtering
- **Hard blocklist:** iptv-org `blocklist.json`, `is_nsfw`, the `xxx` and `religious` categories, closed channels, and streams with a known end date.
- **[v2] Source policy (new):** a stream is used only if its host is (a) the channel's own website domain, (b) a known official broadcaster/FAST distribution CDN (Amagi, Wurl, Tubi, Ottera, broadcasters' own Akamai/CloudFront endpoints, …), or (c) a known redirector to official FAST services. Known third-party restream hosts are excluded. The rules live in `config/curation.json` and are reviewed by a person.
- **[v2] Browser-playable only:** a stream must be HTTPS (an HTTPS site cannot load HTTP video), must send CORS headers that allow our site, and must not need a custom User-Agent or Referrer (browsers can't set those).
- **6 categories:** Animations, TV Series, Sitcoms, Movies, Sports, News (mapped from iptv-org categories in config).
- **News caps:** at most 2 international channels (default BBC News, DW) and up to 3 Philippine channels.
  - **[v2] Reality check:** PTV-4 and UNTV are **not** in the iptv-org database as of 2026-11-17. The Philippine news candidates there are Abante TV, Bilyonaryo News Channel and CLTV36. PTV/UNTV can be added by hand to `config/curation.json` once official HTTPS streams are confirmed.
- **Dead-link removal:** every 6 hours. **[v2] Caveat:** checks run from GitHub's US servers, so a channel that's fine there may be geo-blocked in the Philippines (and the reverse). To cover this, the player also skips channels that fail on the viewer's own device and remembers them for 24 hours.

### B. EPG timeline (1 past → live now → 2 next)
Unchanged design. **[v2]** Shown only for channels with guide data; others show "No guide available" instead of a broken bar.

### C. Optional onboarding and editable profile
Skippable 3-question wizard (genres, decades, local PH content). The answers become weighted tags in localStorage that reorder the home rows. **[v2]** Ships without an AI call; an AI step can be added later for free-text answers ("I like old Filipino comedies").

### D. Show reminders
- **[v2] Honest scope:** v1 promised browser push "24 h, 1 h and 15 min before" airtime. Real Web Push to a closed browser needs a push server (later: Worker + VAPID) and does not work in most TV-stick browsers. The free core ships **in-app reminders**: a toast plus a system notification where permitted, while the site is open, with the alert times kept. Telegram/Discord stays a Pro feature that needs the Worker.
- Free tier: up to 3 active reminders (as v1).

### E. 10-foot D-pad UI and playback
Large, high-contrast cards; arrow keys, OK/Enter and Back; also mouse and touch. If a stream fails it retries once, then moves to the next channel in the category. TV-stick launch: bookmark the URL, or scan a QR code shown on the site.

### F. Bring-your-own playlist
1 personal `.m3u` URL stored in localStorage, never sent to our servers. **[v2]** The same HTTPS/CORS limits apply, and the UI says so when a pasted playlist won't play.

## 6. Honest content expectations
- **Included:** free FAST channels (Pluto TV, Samsung TV Plus, Tubi, FilmRise and similar, where listed), public/international news with official free streams, and free sports highlight and niche sports channels.
- **Not included:** live NBA, Disney Channel / Cartoon Network, current blockbusters and premium series. Searching for them shows a "Where to watch officially" card.
- **[v2] FAST availability varies by country.** Some Pluto TV/Samsung TV Plus channels may not play in the Philippines. The site says so instead of implying everything plays everywhere.

## 7. Monetization — tightened
1. **"Where to watch officially" referrals.** **[v2]** Start with plain links (no affiliate tags) to the licensed services. Add affiliate tags only after joining a program that accepts Philippine traffic, and disclose affiliate links on the card. Never invent availability: a title is shown as available only after checking that service.
2. **Hardware affiliates** (streaming sticks, ISDB-T antennas): same disclosure rule.
3. **Pro tier ($1.99/mo or $14.99 lifetime).** **[v2]** Needs a payment processor that supports PH payouts (e.g. PayMongo, Lemon Squeezy, Ko-fi memberships) and the Worker + D1 backend for sync and alerts. Not built in v1. Free core stays free.
4. **Supporter badges** via Ko-fi / Buy Me a Coffee: unchanged.

## 8. Risks to watch
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| A broadcaster objects to a stream | Remove it immediately via `config/curation.json` `denyChannels`; keep a contact link on the site. |
| Third-party guide source disappears | EPG is optional per channel; sources are configurable. |
| Free FAST streams geo-blocked in PH | Local skip-and-remember; label "may not play in your region". |
| GitHub schedule paused after 60 days idle | The workflow commits nothing, so keep the repo active or re-enable it from the Actions tab; moving the job to Cloudflare later doesn't fix this, because of the Worker limits above. |

## 8b. v1.1 additions (2026-11-17)
- **No fake listings.** Streams must deliver a real video segment in the 6-hourly check. Each viewer's device also re-checks the channels it can see and hides failures for 12 hours, and a channel that fails to play is removed immediately instead of the app silently jumping elsewhere.
- **Clear schedule.** Previous / Now playing / Up next / Later, with explicit labels, times, countdowns, episode numbers, genre tags and descriptions. Schedule coverage went from about 35% to about 84% of channels by adding Samsung TV Plus, Plex and Roku listings.
- **Languages.** English is the default, and viewers can add Filipino, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Hindi and others.
- **Favorite shows, movies and stars.** Typo-tolerant autosuggest from the live schedule, with cast lists from Wikidata (CC0). My TV shows when picks air, and for picks that aren't on free TV it links to JustWatch.
- **Captions removed** until channels provide reliable ones.

## 8c. v1.2 additions (2026-11-17)
- **Location-aware guide (no fake listings).** The app detects the viewer's country (Cloudflare's public trace endpoint, falling back to the time zone; changeable in the profile). It hides channels not offered there, using the per-country lists that Pluto TV, Samsung TV Plus and Plex publish, iptv-org's region-lock labels, and local-news-per-country. Pluto TV and Samsung TV Plus aren't offered in the Philippines, so their 300+ channels no longer appear there.
- **No VPN.** Routing video through other countries would get around broadcasters' licensing and turn us into a re-streamer: legally risky, and bandwidth costs money. We filter by location instead.
- **Language you actually hear.** A channel counts as English only if English is its main audio track. Schedules written in another language either relabel the channel (exact match) or are dropped (name match).
- **No country gets priority.** Local news follows the viewer's location.
- **Stars directory.** About 3,800 well-known actors, comedians and presenters from Wikidata, so any star can be picked even when nothing of theirs airs today.
- **Phone remote without an account.** The TV shows a QR code and a 6-letter code, and the phone connects peer-to-peer (WebRTC; PeerJS's free public broker only introduces them).
- **Android app.** A Capacitor shell around the web app, adding notifications that fire even when the app is closed. Every build is made by GitHub Actions, scanned with ClamAV (never published if anything is found), and published with a SHA-256 checksum and a VirusTotal link on `download.html`.

## 8d. Email reminders and marketing consent (built 2026-11-17; sender: Gmail via Google Apps Script; auth by Google-signed ID token, no shared secret)
- Email reminders need a server: something to store addresses and send at the right time. That's the Cloudflare Worker + D1 phase, plus an email sender.
- Consent rules we follow (Philippine Data Privacy Act; GDPR for EU visitors; CAN-SPAM): reminder emails never require marketing consent. Marketing is a **separate, unticked** checkbox that names who will email (e.g. "Web TV Hub and [named sister brands]"), with a one-click unsubscribe in every email and a privacy notice saying exactly how addresses are used. No bundled or disguised consent: it's invalid under these laws and the list would be legally unusable.

## 9. Delivery plan
- **v1 (this build):** static site on GitHub Pages; 6-hourly Actions refresh; curation + source policy + health check; EPG for FAST channels; D-pad UI; onboarding; favorites; 3 reminders; BYO playlist; where-to-watch card.
- **v1.1 (done):** QR "open on another screen"; installable PWA; items in 8b. Still open: Cloudflare Pages mirror.
- **v2:** Worker + D1 for cloud sync, Web Push, Telegram/Discord; Pro tier payments.
