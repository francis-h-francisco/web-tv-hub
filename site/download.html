Web TV Hub for Android

The same free TV, plus show reminders that pop up even when the app is closed, and it doubles as a remote for Web TV Hub on your TV.

Never miss a show

Set a reminder and your phone alerts you an hour before, 15 minutes before and when it starts, even if the app is closed. The website can only remind you while it's open.

Cast to your TV

Have a Chromecast or Google TV? Tap the cast button at the top, pick your TV, and the channel plays there while your phone becomes the remote: change channels, pause, and use your phone's volume buttons for the TV's volume.

Your phone becomes the TV remote

Open Web TV Hub on your TV's browser, scan its code, and change channels, volume or pause from your phone. No account needed.

Opens like an app

One tap from your home screen, full screen, no browser bars. It's small (about 4 MB) and always up to date, because the TV guide updates itself.

How to install

  1. Tap Download above and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (this app isn't on Google Play yet).
  3. Tap Install, then open Web TV Hub.
  4. Allow notifications when asked, so show reminders can reach you.

“App not installed as package conflicts with an existing package”? You have an early version (1.4 or older) that used a temporary key. Uninstall Web TV Hub once, then install this one. From version 1.6 on, updates install over the old app and keep your settings.

What the app can access

Internet (to play channels) and notifications (for reminders you set). No contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. Your profile, favorites and reminders stay on your phone.

Prefer not to install anything? Open Web TV Hub in your browser and use your browser's menu → “Add to Home screen”.
