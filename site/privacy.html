Privacy

Effective 17 November 2026. Web TV Hub is a free guide to live TV channels that broadcasters already stream for free. It's run by the same independent team as Norvicka Studio and Pivory Systems. This page explains, in plain words, what we collect and why.

We collect nothing personal

  • Your settings (profile, favourite shows and stars, favorites, reminders, history, kids mode, playlist) stay in your browser or app. We never receive them.
  • "Does this channel play?" reports. To hide channels that don't work where you are, your device anonymously sends the channel's ID and whether it played. Your country comes from your network. We store a daily-changing, one-way scrambled code instead of your IP address, so reports can't be linked to you, and old reports are discarded.
  • Visitor statistics. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies or tracking you across sites.
  • "Where to watch" links. When you tap a link to a paid service, we count the tap (just the service, the day and your country). Some of these links are affiliate links: the service may tell its affiliate partner that a visitor came from Web TV Hub, under that service's own privacy policy.
  • Phone remote. Your phone and TV connect directly. A public introduction service (PeerJS) only passes a random 6-letter code.

Your choices and rights

We hold no account or contact details about you. Your settings live on your own device, and clearing your browser data (or uninstalling the app) removes them. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, and similar laws where you live, you can access, correct, object to processing, withdraw consent and ask for deletion. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

See also our Terms & disclaimer.

Changes

If we ever add optional features that need personal details (such as email reminders), we'll update this page first and ask for your clear consent before collecting anything.
