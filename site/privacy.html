Privacy

Effective 17 November 2026. Web TV Hub is a free guide to live TV channels that broadcasters already stream for free. It's run by the same independent team as Norvicka Studio and Pivory Systems. This page explains, in plain words, what we collect and why.

Without an email address, we collect nothing personal

  • Your settings (profile, favourite shows and stars, favorites, reminders, history, kids mode, playlist) stay in your browser or app. We never receive them.
  • "Does this channel play?" reports. To hide channels that don't work where you are, your device anonymously sends the channel's ID and whether it played. Your country comes from your network. We store a daily-changing, one-way scrambled code instead of your IP address, so reports can't be linked to you, and old reports are discarded.
  • Visitor statistics. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies or tracking you across sites.
  • Phone remote. Your phone and TV connect directly. A public introduction service (PeerJS) only passes a random 6-letter code.

If you choose email reminders

  • What we keep: your email address, time zone and country, the reminders you asked us to email, and a record of your consent (the exact wording you agreed to, when, and a scrambled network code).
  • Why: to email you about an hour before the shows you chose. We send a confirmation email first and do nothing until you confirm.
  • Offers (only if you ticked the separate box): occasional updates and offers from Web TV Hub and its sister brands, Norvicka Studio and Pivory Systems. Unticked by default, never required for reminders, and you can stop offers alone and keep your reminders.
  • We never sell your address or give it to anyone outside the brands named above.
  • Services that handle it for us: Cloudflare (hosting and database) and Google (Gmail, which sends the emails).
  • How long: reminders are deleted a week after the show. Your address is kept until you unsubscribe. After that we keep only the date and fact of unsubscribing, so you're never emailed again by mistake.

Your choices and rights

Every email has one-click links to stop offers or unsubscribe from everything. To see, correct or delete your data, reply to any email from us. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, and similar laws where you live, you can access, correct, object to processing, withdraw consent and ask for deletion. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Changes

If this page changes in a way that affects how we use your email address, we'll ask for your consent again first.
